Frequently asked questions

Is this another form builder?

A form collects text you still have to read. Survey Rocket asks questions in a chat, checks answers as they arrive, and turns results into numbers you can publish.

Where do the answers live?

On the contact record in your own HubSpot portal, and on a dashboard for each survey.

Can we write our own questions?

Yes. The survey asks your questions word for word and never invents its own.

What happens to negative feedback?

It stays private. The permission request only appears after a positive answer, so nothing critical reaches your website.

What does it need from our HubSpot?

A private app connection Lean Labs sets up with you, plus a few contact properties. Your team keeps working in HubSpot as usual.

How fast can we launch?

Setup for a first survey takes TK. To launch, we need your questions and your customer list.
