How it works

Three steps between a question and a published stat.

1

Invite from HubSpot

Your customers get a short email with one link. Reminders go out automatically and stop the moment someone answers.

2

A chat, not a form

Questions arrive one at a time. Almost every answer is a tap, numbers get checked, and a quote ask at the end captures testimonials with permission.

3

Stats you can publish

Every response lands on the HubSpot contact and rolls into running averages. Your dashboard shows which stats are ready for your website.

For when testimonials are not enough

Some audiences cannot give individual testimonials. Survey enough of your customers and you own numbers from your whole customer base that you can stand behind.

Ask the question that produces the stat you want to publish, and let the averages build themselves.
